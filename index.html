PROJECT MISSION

The Challenge in Modern Autonomous Aerial Warfare

Modern combat environments demand autonomous unmanned aerial vehicles capable of making split-second tactical survival decisions without relying on delayed human teleoperation.

The Problem with Traditional Approaches

  • Rigid Rule-Based Vulnerability: Conventional Artificial Potential Field (APF) and geometric PID algorithms fall into local minima when encountering multi-directional threats or high-speed missiles.
  • Teleoperation Latency: Remote pilot communication latency (150ms–500ms) or electronic jamming renders human-in-the-loop evasion obsolete against modern Surface-to-Air Missiles (SAM).
  • Aerodynamic Envelope Violations: Heuristic controllers often demand impossible roll-rates or G-forces, inducing aircraft stall, structural overload, or complete loss of control.
  • Inability to Generalize: Static algorithms fail when threat trajectories, sensor noise, or wind gusts deviate from predetermined lab models.

The ERDENIS Solution: Deep Reinforcement Learning

  • Continuous Action Policy Optimization: Leveraging actor-critic algorithms (PPO & SAC) to synthesize optimal non-linear 3D evasion vectors in real time.
  • Realistic Flight Envelope Constraints: Reward shaping penalizes excessive load factors ($n_z$), angle of attack ($\alpha$) excursions, and sideslip, ensuring airframe structural integrity.
  • AirSim & Gazebo High-Fidelity Simulation: Trained across tens of thousands of dynamic combat scenarios with photorealistic sensory inputs and realistic aerodynamic drag.
  • Mission Preservation vs. Evasion Balance: The agent does not simply flee aimlessly; it maximizes survival probability while recovering the optimal heading toward mission objectives.
SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

Four-Tier Decision & Evasion Pipeline

From raw sensory perception to aerodynamic actuation, ERDENIS executes a mathematically bounded decision pipeline at 50Hz.

01

Perception & Threat Space

Sensory & RWR

Aggregates Radar Warning Receiver (RWR) azimuth, missile closing velocity ($V_c$), line-of-sight (LOS) rate, and adversarial UAV telemetry through Kalman filtering.

Inputs: 24-dim state vector
02

Kinematic Envelope Guard

Aerodynamics

Calculates instant G-limits, maximum available roll rate, dynamic pressure ($q$), and stall boundaries based on the current airspeed and flight altitude.

Constraint: -3.0G ≤ nz ≤ +9.0G
03

DRL Evasion Engine

PPO / SAC Policy

Evaluates the optimal 3D evasion trajectory (barrel roll, split-S, break turn, or low-altitude masking) using deep neural networks trained with custom multi-objective rewards.

Inference: < 20ms execution
04

Autopilot Actuation

PX4 / ArduPilot

Translates target bank angle, pitch rate, and throttle commands into control surface deflections and thrust vectors delivered directly to the flight controller.

Interface: MAVLink / ROS 2
